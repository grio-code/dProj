Privacy Policy

Draft for legal review. Verify actual data practices before publication. Effective date: To be established before public launch.

1. Information handled

Integrate may process account information such as names, usernames, email addresses, profile photos, workspace membership and roles, and customer-provided project records, punch lists, and related content.

2. Purposes

Information is used to authenticate accounts, operate workspaces, show authorized project information, maintain security, and support requested functionality.

3. Service providers

The current application code uses Firebase authentication and Firestore services. Before publication, verify all actual hosting, analytics, storage, logging, email, and other service providers and describe any relevant disclosures.

4. Sharing and access

Workspace information may be available to authorized workspace members according to account permissions. Disclosures to vendors and legal authorities, if applicable, must be accurately documented.

5. Retention and deletion

Retention periods, account deletion processes, backup retention, and data export options must be defined and documented before launch.

6. Security

Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be implemented. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Privacy rights

Applicable privacy laws may provide rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain information about personal data. Add a verified request method and jurisdiction-specific disclosures before publication.

8. Children

Define the intended age eligibility and handling of children’s information before public launch.

9. Contact and updates

Publish the responsible legal entity, contact email, effective date, and policy update process before launch.
